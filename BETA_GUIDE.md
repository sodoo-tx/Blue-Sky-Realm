# Blue Sky Realm: Beta Tester Guide

Thank you for testing. This guide covers how to play, what to look at, and how to send feedback.

## Getting started
- Open the game link, or run **Blue Sky Realm.exe**. No install is needed.
- **Sign up** on the first screen. Pick any name and a password of at least 6 characters. Your save lives in this browser or on this PC, under your account.
- Press **Play**. The game starts with a short *First steps* tutorial and an **Adventurer's Guide** (press **F1** any time).

## What we most want you to test
1. **The first hour.** Is it clear what to do? Where did you get stuck or bored? Note how long you had played.
2. **Save and reload.** Close the game, open it again, press **Continue**. Is everything still there (items, gear, map pins, favourites)?
3. **Combat.** Guard and parry. Does the timing feel fair? Do enemies feel too weak or too strong?
4. **The Endless Rift.** Press Enter and type `rift`. There are four boss types (Juggernaut, Stormcaller, Hivemind, Ramhorn). Is tier 1 fair? How far could you climb?
5. **World wardens.** Type `warden` to find one. Watch the red circles. Hit the weak point when it is exposed.
6. **Signature items (✨).** Wardens, dungeon bosses and the rift drop them. Do their effects feel good and readable? Are any overpowered?
7. **Crafting and the Pack.** Search, favourites (right-click an item), the ×5 / Max queue, and the map pins (Alt-click the map).
8. **Ascension (;).** Do Paragon points, Bestiary tiers and the three daily Trials feel rewarding? Is anything too slow or too generous?
9. **The Forge (\) and the Hollow Reach.** Is tempering/reforging worth the coins? In the Reach (whisper `reach`), are the elites and the Hollow King fair, and are the drops exciting?
10. **Navigator (,), map filters and Ego gear (').** Does the arrow/beam lead you correctly? Do the map filters tame the clutter? Do ego items level at a good pace and feel worth using?
11. **Classes, grounds, species.** At level 10 can you find a training ground (N for rumours, , for the Navigator)? Do masters, starter weapons, training golems and proficiency (damage up, cooldown down) feel good? Do species evolutions feel reachable? Are rare classes too strong for how far they are?
12. **The Initiation and master tasks.** At level 10 does the instructor's pointer and the six steps make the first class clear? Do the daily master tasks (Tasks tab) feel worth doing, and does rapport lowering prices matter?
13. **Mastery trials.** At class level 10, 20 and 30 (the Trials tab at a master of your family): is the gauntlet fair, is the reward signature skill exciting, and does yielding feel safe?
14. **Performance.** Note your device and the quality setting (K, or whisper `quality auto`). Report stutters and where they happened.

## Sending feedback
Press **F2** (or click **BETA · F2 feedback** at the bottom right).
1. Choose a type and describe what happened.
2. Click **Copy report** or **Save as file**.
3. Send it to the developer.

The report attaches your build, device, quality setting, position, gear, rift progress, recent game messages and any errors automatically. **Nothing is sent over the internet by the game itself.**

## Known limits
- Boss music and sound effects were built without being heard by the developer. Tell us how they sound.
- The first rift and warden difficulty were tuned with a computer-controlled test player. Human feedback is needed.
- Bosses reuse monster models with a new look (glow, spikes, aura).
- Menus (Pack, map, crafting) need a mouse or keyboard; a controller covers gameplay only.
- Low-end phones may run slowly. Try `quality low`.
