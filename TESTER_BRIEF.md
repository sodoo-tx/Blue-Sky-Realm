# Tester brief (read this first, 2 minutes)

**Play it:** run `Blue Sky Realm.exe` (nothing to install), or open https://sodoo-tx.github.io/Blue-Sky-Realm/ in a browser.
**Sign up** on the first screen (any name of 3-16 letters/numbers, password of 6+ characters). Your save belongs to that account on that device.

## Keys you will use
| Key | What |
|---|---|
| WASD, mouse | move, look |
| F or right-click, Shift, Space, C | attack, sprint, jump, roll |
| E | interact (talk to masters, open chests, gather) |
| Z X V 0 | class skills |
| `,` | Navigator (arrow and beam to quests, places, items) |
| N / M / I | classes and training grounds / map / pack |
| `[` | Hero sheet and talent tree |
| `\` | menu with search |
| Enter, then type a word | whispers: `classes`, `species`, `legends`, `style`, `souls`, `pack`, `faith`, `pacts`, `blood`, `rift`, `warden`, `guide` |
| F1 / F2 | guide / **send feedback** |

## Please test, in this order
1. **The first hour.** Is it clear what to do? Note how long you had played when you got stuck or bored.
2. **Reach level 10 and follow the instructor.** Is it clear how to get your first class? Did the six Initiation steps make sense?
3. **A second class family.** Visit a different training ground. Is the family's meter (the bar above your hotbar) understandable? Press Enter and type `style` (or `souls`, `pack`, `faith`, `pacts`, `blood`) to read it.
4. **Master tasks, drill and mastery trial** (class level 10). Fair? Fun? Grindy?
5. **Save and quit, reopen, Continue.** Is everything still there (class, skills, grounds, items)?
6. **Combat feel.** Do skills feel good? Is any class far too strong or too weak? Tell us the class name.
7. **Anything hidden.** If you stumble on a legendary monument (whisper `legends` for riddles), tell us how you found it and whether the guardian was fair.
8. **Speed.** Note your device and quality setting (whisper `quality auto`). Report stutters and where.

## How to report
Press **F2** in the game, describe what happened, and click **Copy report** or **Save as file**, then send it to the developer. The report attaches your build, device, quality, position, gear, recent messages and any errors automatically. Nothing is sent over the internet by the game itself.

## Good to know
- It is a beta: expect rough edges. Balance numbers are first guesses.
- Press **H** to hide the screen UI, **F8** to mute.
- Legendary classes exist once per save; do not worry if you never find one.
